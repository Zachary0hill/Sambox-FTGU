  1. Content type: the only solid block on the card (TYPEFILL). It is how people scan a column, so nothing else competes with it.
  2. Job # and actions: job number in faint tabular figures. The ⋯ button appears on hover or focus and opens the quick menu.
  3. Title: 15 / 600, clamped to two lines. The whole card is the click target (stretched link); Enter opens it from the keyboard.
  4. Status + flags: status uses the soft tone with a dot, no “Status:” prefix. Flags only appear when something needs a person: a revision, a sponsor waiting, a blocked step.
  5. Pipeline: one segment per step (Script, Film, Edit, Thumbnail, Sponsor when there is one, Publish). Green done, blue in progress, red needs revision, gray not started. Same data and colors as the stepper in the modal.
  6. People and counts: editor and designer avatars (an empty + ring when a role is unassigned), comments with a red dot when there are unread ones, files, checklist progress. Zero counts are hidden.
  7. Next date: the single date that matters next for this viewer's role (Film, Edit due, Thumb due, then Pub). Red when late, amber when today. The footer is not drawn at all when it would be empty.
