# Ideas & Research board workflow

Use one board for manual and AI-generated ideas. The column tells the team what editorial decision comes next. A separate status on the card shows whether an AI research run needs attention.

## Recommended columns

| Column | What belongs here | Next action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Idea Inbox** | A rough title, observation, link, or AI suggestion. One line is enough to capture it. | Choose an idea to develop, request research, or send a clear pitch straight to review. |
| **In Development** | An idea the team wants to pursue that still needs a sharper hook, a clearer audience, or supporting evidence. | Refine the pitch or give an AI run a specific question to answer. Move the idea to review when the pitch is ready. |
| **Ready for Review** | A pitch with a clear hook and audience, plus sources for claims its premise depends on. | A reviewer approves it, returns it to development with a question, or parks it for later. |
| **Approved** | A person has decided the team should make the content. | Hand the pitch and its supporting research to scripting or production. |

## Routing manual and automated ideas

- **Manual capture.** Send a rough note to **Idea Inbox**. A fully formed pitch can go straight to **Ready for Review**.
- **Automated discovery.** Send a title-only suggestion to **Idea Inbox**. If a run delivers a reviewable pitch with supporting sources, send it to **Ready for Review**.
- **Research on an existing idea.** Attach the findings to the original card and preserve its origin. Move it to **Ready for Review** once the findings answer the open question and the pitch meets the review criteria.

For example, “Why Streetwear Brands Go From Cool to Cringe” can start as a manual note in **Idea Inbox**. In **In Development**, the owner can ask AI to find two documented examples of audiences turning away from a streetwear brand. If the examples support a specific hook, the owner can send the pitch and sources to **Ready for Review**.

## Card details and saved views

| Detail | Recommended behavior |
| --- | --- |
| **Idea origin** | Show `Manual` or `AI-generated` based on who first proposed the idea. A manual pitch keeps its origin when AI helps research it. |
| **Research run** | When the idea has a run, show `Queued`, `Running`, `Complete`, or `Failed`. A failed run gets a **Retry** action. |
| **Parked** | Keep “maybe later” ideas accessible through a saved view so the four board columns stay focused on active editorial work. |
